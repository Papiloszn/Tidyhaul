About Tidyhaul Nigeria Enterprises

Professional cleaning. Dependable service.

Tidyhaul is a janitorial and commercial cleaning company focused on helping businesses and commercial facilities maintain clean, hygienic and professional environments.

Who we are

Supporting cleaner, better-maintained spaces.

Our primary focus is professional janitorial services for businesses and commercial facilities. We also provide office and facility cleaning, deep and specialised commercial cleaning, and selected residential cleaning services.

We understand that dependable cleaning is about more than appearance. It calls for people who take their responsibilities seriously, clear expectations and a consistent approach to maintaining hygienic environments.

Our aim is to build service relationships grounded in accountability, communication and reliable delivery.
